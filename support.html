Support

A few clear answers for your monthly check-in.

How do I get started?

Add an account and enter its current value. Set your FIRE assets and withdrawal rate, then FIRE Paycheck can show a monthly planning value.

How can I update my assets quickly?

Previous balances and quantities are carried forward from the previous month. Update only the values that changed, then save the monthly snapshot.

How does App Lock work?

Enable App Lock in Settings. FIRE Paycheck then uses authentication supported by the device. Depending on your device, iOS may use Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode. FIRE Paycheck does not receive or store biometric or passcode data.

How is Monthly Spendable calculated?

FIRE Assets × Withdrawal Rate ÷ 12. This is a planning value, not financial advice.

How are investment gains calculated?

Current Value − Previous Value − Net Contribution / Withdrawal.

How do Precious Metals work?

Record current value, current quantity, and net contribution or withdrawal for your precious metals accounts.

How do I move my data to a new iPhone?

Export Backup → save the file → on the new device, Import Backup. You choose the save location through the system Files picker.

Does FIRE Paycheck connect to my bank?

No. There is no bank connection.

Does FIRE Paycheck sync automatically through iCloud?

No. FIRE Paycheck is local-first. You can manually export and import backups.

How do I delete all data?

Open Settings → Delete All Data.

Notifications

The reminder requires your permission. You can change notification permissions in iOS Settings.
