Track Both Precious-Metal Value and Quantity

Keep gold, silver, and other quantity-based holdings visible beside the rest of your assets.

Two useful details for one holding

For precious metals, a currency value alone may not tell the whole story. FIRE Paycheck lets you record both a current value and a current quantity. That makes it easier to maintain a personal record of gold, silver, or other holdings measured by weight or units.

Update the information you know

At a monthly check-in, enter the value and quantity you want reflected in your snapshot. You can also record net contributions or withdrawals so a change in your holding is not confused with a change in its value.

No live price claim

FIRE Paycheck does not fetch live gold or silver prices. You supply the current value yourself, based on whichever source and method you prefer. The app is designed for manual tracking, not a real-time quote service.

Keep metals in the wider picture

Precious metals can sit alongside cash, investments, and custom categories in your account structure. Monthly snapshots give you a consistent way to review the overall asset picture while retaining the quantity detail that matters for metals.
