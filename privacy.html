Privacy Policy

Simple, local-first privacy for a personal planning tool.

Last updated: August 31, 2026

1. Overview

FIRE Paycheck is an iPhone app for recording assets and planning a monthly spending value. This policy describes the app’s current V1 behavior.

2. Data stored on your device

Financial information you enter, including accounts, categories, values, quantities, snapshots, contributions, withdrawals, and settings, is stored locally on your device.

3. No FIRE Paycheck account

FIRE Paycheck does not require an account, login, bank connection, or server account. We do not operate a FIRE Paycheck financial-data server.

4. No analytics, ads, or tracking

V1 does not include analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, or third-party tracking services.

5. Notifications

If you enable the Monthly Reminder, the app uses Apple Local Notifications. You control notification permission in iOS Settings. Notifications are scheduled locally.

6. Backup export and import

You may export a local JSON backup and import it later. When exporting, you choose the destination using Apple’s system Files picker, such as On My iPhone or iCloud Drive. FIRE Paycheck does not manage iCloud sync or iCloud backup.

7. System services and Widget

The Small Home Screen Widget shares the necessary local summary data between the app and its Widget Extension through an Apple App Group. V1 does not use CloudKit automatic sync.

8. Data deletion

Use Settings → Delete All Data to remove the app’s stored data. Exported backup files remain wherever you chose to save them and must be deleted separately.

9. Children

FIRE Paycheck is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information.

10. Changes

We may update this policy when the app’s behavior changes. The date above will be updated when this page changes.

11. Contact

For support questions, contact support@example.com. Support email requires user confirmation before publication.
